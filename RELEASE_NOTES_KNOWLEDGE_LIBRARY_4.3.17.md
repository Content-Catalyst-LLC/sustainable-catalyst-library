# Sustainable Catalyst Research Library v4.3.17
## Federated Library Access Foundation

v4.3.17 makes Research Access the first functional experience in the Research Library and establishes the connector architecture for a long-term global library network.

### Public Research Access
The new `[sc_research_access]` surface is intentionally public. Users do not need a university affiliation or a Sustainable Catalyst account to search open launch sources. Accounts remain valuable for later persistence, connected libraries, source management, citations, Workspace continuity, and document production.

### Launch connectors
- Internet Archive — direct public search and item access links.
- MIT Libraries — direct TIMDEX GraphQL discovery across catalog, DSpace@MIT, and ArchivesSpace records.
- Harvard Library — direct LibraryCloud open bibliographic metadata search with digital-resource signals when present.
- Library of Congress — existing public JSON search retained as a direct provider; the registry explicitly preserves the SRU/Z39.50 expansion path.
- UC Berkeley / eScholarship — public repository access surfaced in the launch network; deeper catalog federation is deliberately not misrepresented as complete.

### Access model
Normalized results now distinguish open digital resources from public catalog records and other access states. Sustainable Catalyst provides legitimate routes to resources; institutional credentials remain with the originating library or provider.

### Future architecture made explicit
The page now states the intended direction: public, university, national, and special libraries; local-library discovery; My Libraries; holdings; authenticated digital access; open-copy resolution; and interlibrary-loan/request routes through a reusable connector registry.

### Compatibility
v4.3.16 pathway-aware Research Librarian guidance, v4.3.15 unified search/guided discovery, v4.3.14 front-door boundaries, and v4.3.13 Field Spotlight state remain intact.
