# Energy Systems Intelligence v0.6.0 — Energy Scenario Economics

Adds a bounded economic-analysis layer while preserving v0.5.0 energy balance and all earlier knowledge, numeric, indicator, renewable technology/resource capabilities.

Six executable calculations use explicit assumptions: energy-cost comparison, simple payback, net present value, cost-benefit analysis, cost-efficiency ratios, and a simplified levelized energy-cost estimate. A seventh model defines a portable economic-scenario contract linked to the v0.5.0 energy scenario.

The supplied Sustainable Energy module explicitly calls for energy balance, cost-benefit and cost-efficiency analysis, but does not establish a current price database, financing model, tax/subsidy treatment, or universal economic formula set. v0.6.0 therefore labels its arithmetic as Sustainable Catalyst implementation contracts and requires explicit user assumptions.

No current price, technology cost, discount rate, lifetime, carbon price, financing term, subsidy, tax, escalation rate, non-market benefit, or preferred investment is inferred. Outputs are scenario calculations, not forecasts, financial advice, sustainability scores, technology rankings, or policy recommendations.
