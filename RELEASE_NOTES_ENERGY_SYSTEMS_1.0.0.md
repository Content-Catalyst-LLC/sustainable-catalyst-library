# Release Notes — Energy Systems Intelligence v1.0.0

**Release:** Integrated Sustainable Energy Systems Platform  
**Library:** v5.11.0  
**Carbon & Nature:** v0.5.0  
**Library backend:** v2.16.0

## Added

- Integrated platform registry spanning all nine v0.1.0-v0.9.0 Energy Systems layers.
- Six governed cross-product contracts: Library, Research Librarian, Lab, Workbench, Site Intelligence, Decision Studio.
- Portable integrated-energy-study contract.
- Repository/domain-contract structural certification report.
- GET-only backend and WordPress facade routes for platform framework, contracts, study template, and certification.
- Integrated Platform default UI in `[sc_energy_systems_intelligence]`.
- Machine-readable v1.0.0 platform export and JSON Schemas.

## Preserved

All prior Energy Systems behavior remains present: knowledge objects; v0.2 historical source-bound numeric registry; 30 EISD definitions; renewable technology/resource contracts; energy-balance models; scenario economics; biological carbon/bioenergy integration; live dated World Bank Global Energy observations; neutral decision packets and comparison/readiness models.

## Governance boundaries

v1.0.0 does not infer current factor defaults, official EISD methods, resource suitability, grid adequacy, economic assumptions, biomass carbon neutrality, hidden weights, composite scores, rankings, winners, investments, or policy choices. Cross-product contracts do not mean the separate product runtimes have been modified. Structural certification is not scientific validation or a live deployment audit.

## Migration

No database migration. No new secret. No new API key.
