# Release Notes — Sustainable Catalyst Library v4.7.0

## Institutional & Team Libraries

v4.7.0 adds private, durable organizational curation spaces with owner, steward, editor, contributor, and reader roles; explicit team collections; references-only contributions; optional links to the existing institution and research-unit registries; and bounded activity lineage.

Institution and research-unit bindings reuse existing canonical `sc_institution` and `sc_research_unit` identities. They are contextual only and do not establish legal ownership, employment, institutional membership, authentication, subscriptions, borrowing privileges, or other entitlement.

Team Library membership never exposes a member's My Library, private Research Projects, Collaborative Research Rooms, Reading Notebooks, Evidence Matrices, source binaries, or Workspace state. Only references explicitly contributed to the Team Library are shared.

No automatic publication, evidence promotion, Workspace write, personal-library migration, or private research-content copy is introduced.

The v4.6.0 Research Rooms, v4.5.0 Knowledge Graph & Evidence Intelligence, v4.4.0 Unified Personal Research Environment, and all retained v4.3 storage contracts remain canonical.
