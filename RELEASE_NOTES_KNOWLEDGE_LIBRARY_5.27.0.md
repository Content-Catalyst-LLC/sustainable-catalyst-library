## Identity boundaries

Source clustering is non-destructive. A preferred display record is not a truth or quality authority. Title/name similarity alone never establishes cross-source identity. Duplicate candidates require review. Version families do not imply that the newest record is best or authoritative. No identity relation is treated as evidence truth.
