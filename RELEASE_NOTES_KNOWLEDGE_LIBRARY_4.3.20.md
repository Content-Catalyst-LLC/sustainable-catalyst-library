# Sustainable Catalyst Library v4.3.20
## Open Course Finder Foundation

v4.3.20 adds open learning as a first-class Research Library capability without weakening the access-first layout introduced in v4.3.19.1.

## New public surface

New shortcode:

`[sc_open_course_finder]`

The Research Library page places the Open Course Finder immediately after Research Access and before the Research Librarian.

## Finder behavior

The foundation includes:

- normalized launch-course records;
- keyword search;
- subject filtering;
- access-model filtering;
- explicit access badges;
- query-aware provider gateways;
- mobile/keyboard-friendly presentation;
- no background scraping or undocumented provider APIs.

Access models are intentionally distinct:

- Free & Open
- Free + Certificate
- Free Audit
- Free Preview
- Free + Low-Cost / Mixed
- Verify at Provider

External providers remain authoritative for current enrollment, pricing, credentials, availability, and access terms.

## Sustainability emphasis

The launch catalog includes sustainability and public-interest learning from SDG Academy, FAO, UNITAR/UN DESA, Princeton, Yale, and other providers, alongside computer science and general university learning.

## Access accuracy

The Course Finder does not label Coursera broadly as free. The provider registry identifies Coursera as free-preview by default because Coursera replaced the former general audit experience with a first-module preview model in 2025, while course-specific free and financial-aid routes may still exist.

Similarly, edX is represented as free-audit where a course offers the audit track, with paid verified credentials treated separately.
