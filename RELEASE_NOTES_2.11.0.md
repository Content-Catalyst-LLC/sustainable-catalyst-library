# Sustainable Catalyst Library Backend v2.11.0

Backend v2.11.0 activates Energy Systems Intelligence v0.5.0 — Energy Balance & Systems Modeling.

It preserves all existing Library, private knowledge, institutional, biomedical, Carbon & Nature v0.5.0, Energy Systems v0.2.0 numeric, v0.3.0 indicator, and v0.4.0 renewable technology/resource behavior while adding GET-only deterministic energy-balance calculations and a portable scenario contract.

The executable v0.5.0 models require explicit user inputs. They do not infer technology efficiencies or capacity factors and do not implement dispatch, storage physics, grid reliability/adequacy, economic optimization, scenario persistence, technology ranking, or policy recommendations.

No PostgreSQL schema migration is required for this release.
