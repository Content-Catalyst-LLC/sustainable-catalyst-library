# v5.6.0 R2 interface contract

## Product hierarchy

The Research Library has three equal first-class entry points:

- **Knowledge Base** — Sustainable Catalyst's indexed corpus, topics, relationships, pathways, provenance and record detail.
- **Library Access** — libraries, university repositories, archives, scholarly metadata systems and legitimate access interpretation.
- **Research Librarian** — guided research framing, bounded site-scoped retrieval, access interpretation and next-step routing.

The complete research system remains available below those front doors.

## Interface density

The six protected capability groups remain:

- Explore Knowledge
- Find & Access Research
- My Research
- Evidence & Analysis
- Collaborate & Connect
- Produce & Preserve

R2 shows one group at a time. Opening a protected legacy anchor automatically selects the group containing that capability.

Heavy applications mount in a same-origin iframe with a bounded viewport. This preserves WordPress authentication, existing application assets and APIs while preventing tools such as Open Course Finder from adding thousands of vertical pixels to the public page.

## No capability substitution

R2 does not replace Research Access, Public Library Network, Access Intelligence, Research Librarian, Research Rooms, Team Libraries, Global Federation, Citation Studio, Document Builder, Evidence Matrix, Knowledge Graph, Workspace or the other protected tools with summaries. The existing shortcodes remain canonical and are opened on demand.
