# Sustainable Catalyst Library v4.3.18
## Scholarly Research Connectors & University Access

Research Access is now visibly broader than library catalog search. v4.3.18 promotes scholarly discovery and university research into the first functional surface of the Research Library.

### Direct public search connectors
- Internet Archive
- MIT TIMDEX
- Harvard LibraryCloud
- Library of Congress
- University College Dublin Research Repository (DSpace)
- OpenAlex
- Crossref
- DataCite
- PubMed
- PubMed Central
- Europe PMC
- arXiv

### Compliant gateway
Google Scholar remains a human-directed outbound research gateway. Sustainable Catalyst does not scrape Google Scholar.

### University & Sustainability Research Network
The front door now visibly registers Stanford, Yale, Princeton, Columbia, UC Berkeley, University of Copenhagen, Stockholm University / Stockholm Resilience Centre, Wageningen University & Research, Lund University, ETH Zurich, Oxford, Cambridge, IIASA, United Nations University, and Stockholm Environment Institute. Capabilities are explicitly labeled as direct connector, open repository, API target, or research gateway.

### Public-access principle
University affiliation is not required to search public sources. Authentication for licensed resources remains with the originating library or institution.

### Compatibility
The release preserves v4.3.17 Research Access, v4.3.16 pathway-aware Librarian guidance, v4.3.15 Search/Librarian bridging, Field Spotlight persistence, Publications compatibility, and user confirmation before Workspace mutations.
