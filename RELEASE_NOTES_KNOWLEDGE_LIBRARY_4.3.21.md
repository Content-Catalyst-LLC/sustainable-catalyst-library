# Sustainable Catalyst Library v4.3.21

## Course Access Intelligence & Learning Pathways

v4.3.21 deepens Open Course Finder without expanding the main Research Library page again.

### Added

- course-to-Knowledge-Pathway mappings
- pathway, level, duration, and My Learning filters
- optional course metadata for duration, pace, language, prerequisites, and credentials
- account-owned `Planned`, `In progress`, and `Completed` course states
- direct course-context handoff into the Research Librarian
- deterministic Research Librarian course recommendations
- new Research Librarian `learn` intent and Open Course Finder route
- course recommendation diagnostics

### Preserved

Public course discovery does not require an account. Only personal learning-plan persistence requires sign-in.

The University of Copenhagen SDG course remains a course-level verified free course while Coursera remains a mixed/free-preview provider at the provider level.

No external provider passwords are stored.

The v4.3.18.1 Publications recovery, v4.3.19 library-access layer, v4.3.20 Course Finder foundation, and v4.3.20.2 editorial pathway index remain intact.
