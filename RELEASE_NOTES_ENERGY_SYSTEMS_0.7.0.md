# Energy Systems Intelligence v0.7.0 — Release Notes

**Release:** Biological Carbon & Bioenergy Integration  
**Library:** v5.11.0  
**Carbon & Nature:** v0.5.0  
**Library backend:** v2.13.0

v0.7.0 activates a governed integration layer for bioenergy and biological-carbon questions. It adds five feedstock classes, six pathway definitions, six validated Carbon & Nature bridges, four explicit-input calculators, and a portable bioenergy-carbon scenario contract. Existing Energy Systems knowledge, numeric registry, EISD indicators, renewable-technology/resource model, energy-balance models, and scenario-economics calculations are preserved.

No universal bioenergy yield, methane fraction, feedstock heating value, biochar carbon/stability fraction, biomass-to-oil yield, lifecycle factor, avoided-emissions factor, current dataset, or carbon-credit rule is embedded. Biomass carbon neutrality is explicitly not assumed.

The v2.13.0 Contabo upgrader also repairs the recurring `/opt/sustainable-catalyst/backups` ownership problem when necessary before taking the pre-deployment backup.

No database migration or new credential is required.
