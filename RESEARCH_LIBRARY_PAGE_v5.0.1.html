Research Library

Search knowledge, find legitimate access, and move from discovery into a unified private research environment. v5.0.1 hardens the connected public research layer with a bounded ten-scenario first-party production soak, explicit safe-route caching, cache/freshness observability, malformed-request guards, and integration diagnostics. The underlying public connections remain explicit, one-hop, read-only, and separate from private research and governance.

Research across Sustainable Catalyst, libraries, university repositories, scholarly databases, archives, open digital collections, and open learning. Public discovery remains open to everyone; signed-in users use the same Sustainable Catalyst account across My Library, saved research continuity, private Research Projects, private research tools, and Workspace. Personal collections and recommendations remain separate from Sustainable Catalyst's official editorial selections.

Personal Research Environment

Resume the Work, Not the Interface

Open one private, account-scoped view of your current research state. v4.4.0 composes the records already stored in My Library, Saved Research, Research Projects, Source Bundles, Reading Notebooks, Evidence Matrices, Open Learning II, Workspace continuity, Research Librarian II, and Research Portability. It does not migrate those records into a replacement database or duplicate their private contents. v4.5.0 adds Knowledge Graph & Evidence Intelligence as another composition layer over those same canonical records, not as a second research database. v4.6.0 adds project-anchored Research Rooms, v4.7.0 adds durable Team Libraries without migrating personal research into an organizational store, and v4.8.0 adds an explicit metadata-federation layer that publishes only selected Team Library references. v4.9.0 adds a public integration facade over already-public Library records without changing the private storage or permissions of the research environment. v5.0.0 composes that public facade with explicit public knowledge, pathway, publication-graph, and federation relationships; v5.0.1 hardens that composition under bounded production-soak scenarios without exposing or migrating private research.

[sc_personal_research_environment title="Unified Personal Research Environment"]

Collaborative Research Rooms

Share the Research Context Without Sharing the Entire Private Project

Create a private room around one Research Project, add Sustainable Catalyst account members with explicit roles, and share only the references needed for review. Rooms keep human-authored review notes, decision records, and activity lineage. Joining a room does not make someone a project owner, expose My Library or personal notebooks, copy private source binaries, publish research, promote evidence, or write into Workspace.

[sc_collaborative_research_rooms title="Collaborative Research Rooms"]

Institutional & Team Libraries

Build a Durable Shared Library Without Absorbing Personal Research

Create a private team-curation space, assign owner, steward, editor, contributor, and reader roles, organize explicit team collections, and contribute references with provenance. A Team Library may link to an existing Sustainable Catalyst institution or research unit, but that link is contextual rather than proof of legal ownership, membership, subscription entitlement, or access rights. My Library, private projects, Research Rooms, notebooks, matrices, and source binaries remain private unless a member deliberately contributes a safe reference.

[sc_institutional_team_libraries title="Institutional & Team Libraries"]

Global Research Federation

Exchange Research Metadata Without Federating Private Research

Publish checksummed, references-only manifests from governed Team Libraries; expose a compatible-node discovery document; quarantine inbound remote manifests; and explicitly accept approved metadata into a Team Library while preserving remote node and manifest provenance. Federation peer trust governs transport and review rather than truth. Remote node identity, institutional context, and federation participation never prove membership, subscription entitlement, legal authority, or access rights.

[sc_global_research_federation title="Global Research Federation"]

Library API, Embeds & Interoperability

Make Public Knowledge Reusable Without Making Private Research Public

Use versioned public object profiles, canonical URLs, provenance, checksummed interoperability manifests, and read-only embed cards for already-published Library records. External embeds require an explicitly allowed origin and send no credentials. The facade does not expose raw WordPress post meta, My Library, private projects, notebooks, Evidence Matrix bodies, Research Room or Team Library membership, private federation governance, or Workspace state.

[sc_library_api_interoperability title="Library API, Embeds & Interoperability"]

Connected Public Research Infrastructure

Move From a Public Record Into Its Declared Research Context

Connect already-public Library objects to explicit public topics, concepts, named entities, sources, claims, Knowledge Pathways, Publication ↔ Research Graph links, and published federation metadata. v5.0.1 adds bounded production-soak diagnostics, explicit safe-route caching, cache/freshness headers, and malformed-request hardening. The network remains one hop and provenance-labeled; it does not infer semantic similarity, expose private project graphs, or turn Team Library or Research Room membership into public data.

Saved Research

Keep Unfinished Research in Motion

Save searches you want to repeat, keep passive watchlists of topics, providers, sources, authors, institutions, collections, or courses you want to revisit, and maintain a private queue of research questions, sources, searches, datasets, and tasks. Watchlists in v4.3.29 are reminders, not background monitoring or automatic alerts.

[sc_research_continuity title="Saved Research & Queue"]

Research Projects

Organize Research Around the Work It Serves

Create private research projects that connect the records you already own or can legitimately access: Citation Studio sources, My Library items, saved searches, watchlists, research-queue entries, source collections, document drafts, courses, Knowledge Pathways, and external references. Build project-level source bundles from those links without copying source content or private files into another silo.

[sc_unified_research_projects title="Research Projects & Source Bundles"]

Reading & Annotation

Read With the Research Context Beside You

Create private, account-persistent reading notebooks; attach them to a Research Project or references-only Source Bundle; keep source-linked notes, concise reusable excerpts, tags, ordering, and page-, section-, timestamp-, paragraph-, or custom-locator annotations. The older browser-local Research Notebook remains available for compatibility, while this workspace follows the signed-in Sustainable Catalyst account across devices.

[sc_reading_notebook_workspace title="Reading, Notebook & Annotation Workspace"]

Evidence Review

Compare Claims Against the Evidence You Explicitly Link

Promote selected Reading Notes, Source Annotations, canonical Evidence Notes, project references, or external sources into explicit claim/evidence relationships. Review support, qualification, contradiction, context, source diversity, unresolved references, and verification gaps without allowing the system to infer truth or change your claim status or confidence.

[sc_evidence_matrix_workspace title="Evidence Matrix & Claim Intelligence"]

Knowledge Graph & Evidence Intelligence

See the Explicit Research Structure Behind a Project

Project the relationships already recorded in Research Projects, Source Bundles, Reading Notebooks, notes, annotations, Evidence Matrices, claims, evidence sources, and Open Learning II routes into one private graph. Evidence Intelligence summarizes support, contradiction, qualification, context, source diversity, unresolved references, and verification gaps using existing deterministic matrix diagnostics. It does not infer semantic links from private text, score truth, change claim status or confidence, publish research, or write to Workspace.

[sc_knowledge_graph_evidence_intelligence title="Knowledge Graph & Evidence Intelligence"]

Workspace continuity

Carry the Research Context Forward Without Copying the Research

Prepare a signed, references-only continuity packet for Workspace, then return to the same Library project, source bundle, reading notebook, or evidence matrix. The handoff is explicit and account-bound; neither product silently writes into the other.

[sc_library_workspace_continuity title="Library ↔ Workspace Continuity"]

Metadata Governance

Improve Record Quality Without Hiding the Review Decision

Inspect research-source completeness, normalized DOI/ISBN/URL values, existing canonical-source decisions, and named-entity authority records. Exact authority-URI, normalized-label, and alias matches can surface review candidates, but no candidate becomes a canonical entity until an authorized reviewer explicitly accepts the resolution. Original records, aliases, provenance, and historical assignments remain preserved.

[sc_metadata_quality_center title="Metadata Quality & Entity Resolution"]

Open Learning

Open Learning II can sequence selected catalog courses into a transparent route, preserve unknown prerequisite or duration fields as unknown, and save a private route manifest without enrolling you or claiming current provider terms.

Publications ↔ Research Graph

Move From a Public Article Into Its Declared Research Context

Open a Sustainable Catalyst publication and inspect the public graph that editors have explicitly connected to it: Knowledge Topics, canonical Concepts and Named Entities, public Research Sources, public Claims, its Article Map, and published Knowledge Pathways. The public graph never exposes private Projects, Notebooks, Evidence Matrices, My Library records, queues, or source bundles. Signed-in users can add the publication itself to an owned Research Project as a references-only handoff.
