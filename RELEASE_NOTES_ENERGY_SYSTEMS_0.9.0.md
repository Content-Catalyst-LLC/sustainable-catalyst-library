# Release Notes — Energy Systems Intelligence v0.9.0

**Release:** Energy Decision Intelligence  
**Library:** 5.11.0  
**Backend:** 2.15.0  
**Carbon & Nature:** 0.5.0

## Added

- 12 governed Energy Decision criteria across 9 dimensions.
- Portable energy-decision packet contract.
- Neutral alternative comparison matrix.
- Decision packet readiness inspection.
- Unit and period mismatch detection without automatic harmonization.
- Provenance and uncertainty coverage reporting.
- WordPress Decision Intelligence tab and packet editor.
- GET-only REST facade for framework, criteria, packet template, matrix, and readiness.
- JSON Schemas and machine-readable v0.9.0 decision registry.
- Decision Studio handoff refs for packet, matrix, and readiness contracts.

## Preserved

Global Energy Intelligence v0.8.0 remains active, including the World Bank connector contract and source-year/freshness boundaries. Bioenergy/carbon v0.7.0, economics v0.6.0, balance v0.5.0, technologies/resources v0.4.0, indicators v0.3.0, numeric registry v0.2.0, and knowledge foundation v0.1.0 are preserved.

## Guardrails

No automatic normalization, hidden weighting, composite sustainability scoring, alternative ranking, winner selection, investment recommendation, or policy recommendation is enabled. Decision readiness is not a merit score.

No database migration and no new secret are required.
