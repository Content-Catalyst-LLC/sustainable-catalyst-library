# Energy Systems Intelligence v1.4.0 — Energy Modeling & Uncertainty

v1.4.0 activates Lab as the governed uncertainty-analysis layer around the explicit Workbench runtime introduced in v1.3.0. Library declares study structure and provenance, Lab designs seeded uncertainty evaluations and analyzes returned results, and Workbench remains the arithmetic authority.

The certified v1.4 workflow supports Monte Carlo and Latin hypercube designs with explicit uniform, normal, lognormal, or triangular distributions. It reports empirical summary statistics, central intervals, explicit threshold probabilities, and input-influence sensitivity using Pearson, Spearman, and standardized regression diagnostics.

Sensitivity ranking refers only to input influence on the chosen output. It is not technology ranking and does not imply a recommendation.
