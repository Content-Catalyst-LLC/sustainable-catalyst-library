# Energy Systems Intelligence 1.4.0

## Energy Modeling & Uncertainty

- Adds a Library-side Energy Modeling & Uncertainty registry and study template.
- Advances the Lab runtime requirement from 0.101.0 to 0.102.0.
- Certifies Lab routes for framework, planning, analysis, and structural result validation.
- Preserves Workbench 6.2.0 as the explicit calculation authority.
- Requires explicit distributions, seed, sample count, calculation inputs, and result path.
- Prohibits automatic Lab→Workbench execution, hidden defaults, automatic persistence, technology ranking, winner selection, recommendations, market-data substitution, avoided-emissions inference, and carbon-credit claims.
