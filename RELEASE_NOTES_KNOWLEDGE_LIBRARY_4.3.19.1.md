# Sustainable Catalyst Library v4.3.19.1
## Access-First Layout & Editorial Compression

v4.3.19.1 is a presentation and information-architecture release. It does not replace or narrow the Research Library's underlying capabilities. Instead, it removes the legacy explanatory density that accumulated before Research Access, the Research Librarian, Knowledge Pathways, Workspace, and connected research tools became functional.

### Main-page changes

The Research Library page is reduced from roughly 700 lines / about twenty major sections to about 190 lines / eight functional sections.

The primary journey is now:

1. Research Access
2. Ask the Research Librarian
3. Research Flow
4. Search Sustainable Catalyst
5. Explore Knowledge
6. Research Workspace
7. Applied Tools
8. Research Infrastructure

### Removed from the main journey

The following older explanatory sections are no longer standalone page sections:

- What This Library Is Built to Do
- How the Library Works
- From Symbols to Systems
- Signature Learning Formats
- Technical Knowledge Systems
- Research Layer
- Research Library Standards
- Library Principles

Their underlying capabilities, editorial standards, repository code, documentation, APIs, and institutional records remain intact.

### Consolidated

Reader Pathways, Featured Knowledge Pathways, and Core Libraries are combined into a single Explore Knowledge section.

Methods, code, Foundation Documents, archive, standards, and the Institutional Research Portal are consolidated into a compact Research Infrastructure section.

### Preserved

- v4.3.19 Global Library Search
- My Libraries and Research Libraries
- Digital Access Resolver
- Internet Archive and scholarly/university connectors
- Research Librarian front door and full interface
- Search ↔ Research Librarian bridge
- Knowledge Pathway recommendations
- Research Workspace
- Workbench / Decision Studio / Site Intelligence / Lab handoffs
- v4.3.18.1 Publications/Field Spotlight integrity recovery
- explicit confirmation before Workspace mutations
