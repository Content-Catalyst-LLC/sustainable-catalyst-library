# Knowledge Library v5.24.0 Release Notes

**Release:** Cross-Publication Evidence Synthesis & Competing Hypotheses  
**Backend:** v2.35.0

### Added
- `sc-library-cross-publication-evidence-synthesis/1.0` payload in corpus knowledge maps.
- Support-connected reviewed structures, explicit contradiction pairs, cross-publication relation summaries, and argument paths.
- Explicit hypothesis nodes and `explicit-hypothesis-membership` edges.
- Explicit competing-hypothesis sets when reviewed candidate metadata supplies `competing_hypothesis_set`/`hypothesis_set`.
- Research Library views: **Evidence Synthesis** and **Competing Hypotheses**.
- Synthesis metrics in the Research Inspector.

### Preserved boundaries
No consensus, hypothesis, competition, causality, or truth inference. Evidence balance is descriptive only. Durable synthesis remains Platform Core-owned.
