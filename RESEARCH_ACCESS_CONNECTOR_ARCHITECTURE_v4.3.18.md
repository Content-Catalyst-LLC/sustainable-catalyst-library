# Research Access connector architecture — v4.3.18

## Capability classes
1. **Direct connector** — Sustainable Catalyst queries an approved machine-readable API.
2. **Open repository** — public institutional repository records and open content routes.
3. **Scholarly metadata** — DOI, citation, institution, topic, and open-access discovery services.
4. **Research gateway** — compliant browser handoff where direct programmatic access is unavailable or not appropriate.
5. **Authenticated access** — reserved for future holdings/entitlement work; credentials stay with the institution.

## Current direct public sources
Internet Archive, MIT TIMDEX, Harvard LibraryCloud, Library of Congress, UCD Research Repository, OpenAlex, Crossref, DataCite, PubMed, PubMed Central, Europe PMC, and arXiv.

## Near-term expansion
Princeton, Columbia, Stanford, Yale, Berkeley, Copenhagen, Stockholm/DiVA, Wageningen, Lund, ETH Zurich, Oxford, Cambridge, IIASA, UNU, SEI, public-library systems, OpenURL/SRU/Z39.50/Primo adapters, holdings, and access resolution.
