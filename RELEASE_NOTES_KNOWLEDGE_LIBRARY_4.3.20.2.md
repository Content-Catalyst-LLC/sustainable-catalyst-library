# Sustainable Catalyst Library v4.3.20.2

## Knowledge Pathway Editorial Index & Course Catalog Refinement

This release replaces the card-heavy Knowledge Pathways presentation introduced in v4.3.20.1 with a restrained editorial index designed to read like an institutional research directory rather than a dashboard.

### Knowledge Pathways

- Replaces the eight boxed question cards with a numbered editorial list.
- Introduces a dedicated **Explore by Field** rail for the five core Library domains.
- Uses rules, spacing, numbering, and typography rather than repeated card containers.
- Preserves all eight question routes and all five core field routes.
- Collapses to a single-column reading order on smaller screens.
- Preserves keyboard focus visibility and reduced-motion behavior.

### Open Course Finder

Adds course-level access verification so a specific course can be classified independently of its hosting platform's general access model.

The first course using this model is:

**The Sustainable Development Goals – A global, transdisciplinary vision for the future**  
University of Copenhagen · Coursera · Beginner · 3 modules · approximately 10 hours

The course is cataloged as **Free Course** while Coursera remains **Free Preview** at the provider level. This prevents a provider-wide assumption from overriding verified course-level access.

Verification references reviewed 2026-08-11:
- University of Copenhagen sustainability course catalog: https://oneplanet.ku.dk/en/courses-link/
- University of Copenhagen continuing education listing: https://continuing-education.ku.dk/sustainability/
- Coursera course: https://www.coursera.org/learn/global-sustainable-development
- Coursera sustainability catalog currently includes the course among free sustainability courses.

### Preserved

Research Access, My Libraries, Digital Access Resolver, Open Course Finder filtering, Research Librarian, Knowledge Pathway recommendations, Workspace, applied tools, Research Infrastructure, Publications recovery, and all retained Field Spotlight behavior remain intact.
